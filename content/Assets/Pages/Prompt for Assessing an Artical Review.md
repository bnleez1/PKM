I wrote a 250–300 word academic article review. Act as a **reviewer and coach, not a writer**. Do not rewrite my review or add information from the original article.

Check whether my review clearly and accurately includes:

1. **Problem and purpose:** the research problem, purpose of the study, and research question(s).
2. **Methodology:** participants/context, research design, and main method(s) of data collection.
3. **Major findings:** the two or three most important findings and, when appropriate, what the authors believe they mean.
4. **Conclusions and relevance:** the authors' main conclusion, an important limitation/implication/recommendation when relevant, and how the article relates to my own research.
5. **Academic writing:** accurate reporting of the authors' ideas, effective paraphrasing, appropriate APA in-text citations, academic register, organization, coherence, and a word count of 250–300 words.

For each area, rate it as **Complete, Partially Complete, or Needs Attention** and briefly explain why.

Then:

- Identify the **three most important improvements** I should make.
- Point to the specific sentence or section that needs attention.
- Ask questions or give hints that help **me make the revision myself**.
- Do **not** rewrite sentences for me or provide replacement sentences.
- Do **not** invent information that is not included in my review.

Finish with a short checklist I can use before submitting the assignment.
