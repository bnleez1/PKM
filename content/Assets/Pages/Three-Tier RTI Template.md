# Three-Tier RTI Template

Use this page as one active RTI record for a student or teacher trainee. Keep the plan focused on **one priority need, one observable goal, and one current level of support**.

> **Practical rule:** Record only the information needed to decide what support to provide next.

## Quick properties

- **Current Tier:** Tier 1 / Tier 2 / Tier 3

- **Next Review:** date

- **Intervention Focus:** short tag or category
