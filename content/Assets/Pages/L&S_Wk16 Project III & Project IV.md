Prope Project IV instructions: Project IV consists of two one-on-one conversations with a classmate(s): one conversation on Thursday, November 23, 2023 and another conversation on Friday, November 24, 2023. Partners and topics to discuss will be chosen randomly and when you are to have the conversation. You will be graded on both conversations, but will be asked to choose which conversation you think went the smoothest and why. Submit your reflection regarding which of your conversations went the best on Friday, November 24, 2023 to [this online form](https://forms.office.com/Pages/ResponsePage.aspx?id=kuLi4dYhSUi3EE1H2VeK0ItjcCXTT1VEvQ9aSPhTcDNUME4zVFNFUDRXU1ZaVkE3R0hSVFBWTFZPMy4u). between 11:00 AM and 11:45 PM, Friday, November 24, 2023. A good conversation includes the following:

1. The conversation lasts for five minutes.

2. Each speaker speaks about the same amount of time.

3. Follow-up questions are used to keep the conversation going, helping each other if the discussion slows down or if one speaker is struggling to find the words to maintain conversational flow.

4. Each conversational pair should choose who should begin the conversation, avoiding repeating the question or topic presented. The topic or question will be given only twice.

Note: No devices, dictionaries, notes, etc. may be used during the conversation, which includes any assistance from anyone other than your speaking partner (e.g., your instructor). Each conversation will be recorded and made available to the student.
