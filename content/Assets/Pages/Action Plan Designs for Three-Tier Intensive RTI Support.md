# Action Plan Designs for Three-Tier Intensive RTI Support

When moving a teacher trainee into Tier 3 support, the focus shifts entirely from generalized instruction to highly individualized, frequent, and intensive coaching. For adult learners tackling complex cognitive tasks—like narrowing down a thesis statement or designing qualitative research methodology—the action plan needs to break massive hurdles into immediate, manageable steps.

Here are three distinct ways to design a Tier 3 individual action plan, depending on where the trainee is getting stuck. You can build any of these directly into your PKM setup as a template.

## Design 1: The Micro-Milestone Tracker

**Best for:** Trainees suffering from "blank page syndrome" or those overwhelmed by the scope of academic writing.

Instead of setting a goal like "Draft the literature review," this design forces you and the trainee to break the intervention down into hyper-specific, weekly deliverables.

| Date       | The Micro-Goal                                                     | The Intervention (1-on-1 Focus)                            | Evidence of Completion                             |
| :--------- | :----------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------- |
| **Week 1** | Formulate a single, testable research question.                    | 30-min coaching on narrowing scope.                        | Trainee submits 3 variations of the question.      |
| **Week 2** | Identify 5 peer-reviewed sources directly related to the question. | Joint database search session demonstrating boolean logic. | An annotated bibliography of just those 5 sources. |
| **Week 3** | Draft the methodology rationale (1 paragraph).                     | Reviewing strong vs. weak rationale examples together.     | A 150-word draft submitted for feedback.           |

## Design 2: The UDL Co-Creation Contract

**Best for:** Practicing transparent teaching and giving adult learners agency over their own intervention.

If a trainee is struggling to synthesize qualitative data, standard written essays might be the barrier, not their actual comprehension of the data. This plan is built *with* the trainee during the initial Tier 3 meeting, actively applying Universal Design for Learning principles.

| Plan Component                   | Details (Co-authored with Trainee)                                                                                                              |
| :------------------------------- | :---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **The Core Bottleneck**          | *Where exactly is the breakdown happening? (e.g., "I understand the interviews, but I can't code the themes.")*                                 |
| **Means of Representation**      | *How will I (the trainer) present this concept differently? (e.g., visual mind maps of data instead of text lists).*                            |
| **Means of Action & Expression** | *How will the trainee demonstrate they get it? (e.g., recording a 5-minute audio walkthrough of their data themes instead of writing a draft).* |
| **Check-in Frequency**           | *E.g., 15 minutes every Tuesday morning.*                                                                                                       |

## Design 3: The Iterative Feedback Loop

**Best for:** Deep-dive academic writing interventions where the trainee needs to learn *how* to implement structural feedback, rather than just fixing grammar.

This design functions as a running log of revisions. It tracks not just what you taught them, but whether they actually applied the specific coaching in their next draft.

| Coaching Session Date | Focused Skill                     | Trainer Feedback Provided                                                    | Trainee Action Required                                                   | Review Date | Status      |
| :-------------------- | :-------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------- | :------------------------------------------------------------------------ | :---------- | :---------- |
| **Oct 12**            | Aligning thesis with methodology. | "Your qualitative methods don't match the quantitative claim in the thesis." | Rewrite thesis statement to reflect qualitative discovery.                | Oct 15      | 🟡 Pending  |
| **Oct 15**            | Paraphrasing vs. Quoting.         | "Too many block quotes. You are losing your academic voice."                 | Convert paragraphs 3 and 4 from direct quotes into synthesized summaries. | Oct 19      | 🟢 Mastered |

---

> **Key insight:** The most common failure point in Tier 3 for higher education is a lack of strict exit criteria. Ensure whichever design you use includes a definitive metric for when the trainee no longer needs 1-on-1 intensive support and can return to Tier 1 or Tier 2.
