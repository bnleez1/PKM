# Gen AI Prompting for generating a newsletter


💡 Prompt below did not generate anything useful. I did, however, come up with a [[Newsletter template]].

# Prompt template 1a - Researching and gathering information

Identify the top six authorities who have designed newsletter templates for educational style newsletters for instructors, trainers, and/or coaches and provide their top six commonly recommended newsletter templates designed for instructors, trainers, and/or coaches. Provide it in a list.

# Prompt template 1b

Do you see any similarities and overlaps in the advice advocated from one authority to the next? Provide them all in a list.

# Prompt template 2 - Expanding the information

For the point (**insert point**) above, in the context of (**topic**): 1) Give a concise explanation of what it is; 2) Explain the benefits and why it's beneficial; 3) Describe the three most important aspects, why they are the most important aspects of that strategy, the role of each important aspect, the process of each important aspect, the benefit of each important aspect, how each important aspect can help you achieve or implement the point; 4) Specify three practical pieces of advice or tips on what action to take; 5) Specify three practical action steps; 6) What likely outcome(s) might there be from taking the action in the context of (topic); and 7) The key takeaway from this point.

# Prompt template 3 - Refining and research for its purpose and target audience

For the point and aspects of (**point 1**) explained above, in terms of (**topic**): Generate a comprehensive and detailed chapter. Illustrate and explain the point and aspects of that point with examples and/or anecdotes that make it more relatable to (avatar). Write it in a way (purpose of content). Use all of the information provided for this point and aspects of this point in the previous responses to create in-depth and informative content.
