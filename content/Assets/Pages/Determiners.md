# Determiners

[Determiners: Articles, Demonstratives, Quantifiers & Possessives | EasyTeaching](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pcYrmHrtLRY)[Determiners: Articles, Demonstratives, Quantifiers & Possessives | EasyTeaching - Notes](../Weblinks/Determiners%20Articles,%20Demonstratives,%20Quantifiers%20&%20Possessives%20%20EasyTeaching.md)

Determiners are words or phrases that precede and modify nouns in a sentence. They provide more information about the noun they accompany, such as quantity, possession, specificity, and definiteness. Determiners play a crucial role in specifying and clarifying the meaning of nouns in context. There are several types of determiners, each serving a distinct purpose.

1. [[(ART) Article (determiner)]]

    - **Definite Article (the):** Refers to a specific noun that is known to both the speaker and the listener.

        - Example: *The car is parked outside.*

    - **Indefinite Articles (a, an):** Refer to non-specific nouns, introducing them to the reader or listener for the first time.

        - Example: *I saw a cat in the garden.*

2. Demonstratives

    - **This, that, these, those:** Point out specific nouns or groups of nouns in relation to the speaker's location and perspective.

        - Examples:

            - *This book is interesting.*

            - *Those flowers are beautiful.*

3. [[Quantifiers]]

    - **All, some, many, few, several, any, each, every:** Indicate the quantity or amount of the noun.

        - Examples:

            - *All students must attend the lecture.*

            - *She bought some new clothes.*

            - *Many people enjoy traveling.*

4. **Possessives**

    - **My, your, his, her, its, our, their:** Indicate ownership or possession of the noun.

        - Examples:

            - *This is my house.*

            - *Her cat is very playful.*

            - *Their car is parked outside.*

5. **Numbers**

    - **One, two, three, first, second, last, next:** Specify the quantity or order of the noun.

        - Examples:

            - *I have two dogs.*

            - *She is the first person in line.*

            - *Next week, we will meet again.*

6. **Distributives**

    - **Each, either, neither, both:** Refer to individual items or members within a group.

        - Examples:

            - *Each student should bring their own supplies.*

            - *Neither option seems suitable.*

7. **Interrogatives**

    - **Which, what, whose:** Used to ask questions about the identity, possession, or type of the noun.

        - Examples:

            - *Which book did you choose?*

            - *What is your favorite color?*

8. **Relative Pronouns** See also [[(PRO) Pronouns]].

    - **Who, whom, whose, which, that:** Introduce relative clauses, providing more information about the noun they modify.

        - Examples:

            - *The person who called left a message.*

            - *This is the book that I was talking about.*

In summary, determiners are essential for specifying and adding context to nouns in English sentences. They encompass articles, demonstratives, quantifiers, possessives, numbers, distributives, interrogatives, and relative pronouns, each serving a unique purpose in conveying information about the noun they modify.
