    - Suggestions for final poetry reading

        - **Avoid** any meta-speak: "Hello, my name is"; "I'm going to read my..."; etc.

        - Only provide the title of your poem, and begin.

        - Read slower than you think you should.

        - Read louder than you think you should.

        - Read with greater intonation (more highs and lows) as you think you should.

        - Read with more pauses than you think you should.
