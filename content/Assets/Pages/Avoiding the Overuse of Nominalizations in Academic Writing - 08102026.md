# Avoiding the Overuse of Nominalizations in Academic Writing
[[Thursday, October 8, 2026]]

### Topic sentences for revision practice

> [!note] Note
> When writing academic texts, try to avoid overusing nominalizations where verbs are less active and dynamic (e.g., overusing linking verbs ([[Linking Verbs The Hidden Connectors of English]]), *to make, to take*, etc.

10. The reduction of unnecessary wording, replacement of vague expressions, and elimination of repetitive statements make an important contribution to the improvement of academic prose.
