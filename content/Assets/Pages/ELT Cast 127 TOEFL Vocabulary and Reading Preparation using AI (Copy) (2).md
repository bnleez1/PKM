# ELT Cast 127: TOEFL Vocabulary and Reading Preparation using AI (Copy)



💡 Use your large language model of choice (CoPilot, ChatGPT, etc.) to help prepare for the reading section of the TOEFL. The paper-based TOEFL (TOEFL Institucional en la UAA) typically consists 30 synonym questions and 30 reading comprehension questions, which should be completed within 45 minutes. Consider the time you have to answer questions when preparing for the TOEFL. See also TOEFL Preparation-UAA.

## Prompt used to review TOEFL reading comprehension

Create a 700-800 word TOEFL reading on the topic of endocrinology and include one question for each of the following question types: main idea, factual question-positive, factual question-negative, inference question-specific application, inference question-general application, analogy question, written expression question, organization question, follow-on question, and viewpoint question.

## Types of reading comprehension questions

- Most frequently asked questions

    - Main idea questions

    - Factual questions - positive

    - Factual questions - negative

    - Inference questions - specific applications

    - Inference questions - general applications

- Less frequently asked questions

    - Analogy questions

    - Written expression questions

    - Organization questions

    - Follow-on questions

    - Viewpoint questions
