### FCE Use of English Practice Test

#### Part 1: Multiple-choice Cloze

For questions 1-8, read the text below and decide which answer (A, B, C or D) best fits each gap.

*One of the best ways to learn English is to immerse yourself in the language. When you (1) ___ new vocabulary, it’s important to use it frequently. If you (2) ___ using new words regularly, you’ll (3) ___ them more quickly. Additionally, (4) ___ conversations with native speakers can significantly (5) ___ your fluency. Another effective method is (6) ___ watching English films or reading books, as these activities help you understand how words (7) ___ naturally in context. Above all, patience and (8) ___ are essential for mastering any language.*

| :- | :-------- | :---------- | :---------- | :---------- |

#### Part 4: Key Word Transformation
