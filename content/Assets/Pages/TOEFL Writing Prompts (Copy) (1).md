# TOEFL Writing Prompts (Copy)

- Do you agree or disagree with the statement that it's better to live in a city than in a rural area?

- Some people prefer to work in teams, while others prefer to work alone. Which do you prefer and why?

- Should students have a part-time job while studying at university?

- Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of using technology in the classroom.

- What are the most essential qualities of a good leader? Provide examples to support your answer.

- Some people believe that the best way to learn a foreign language is by immersing themselves in a country where the language is spoken. Do you agree or disagree?

- Should governments invest more in public transportation systems?

- Is it better for children to grow up in a large or small family?

- Discuss the impact of social media on communication and relationships.

- Is it essential for people to continue learning new things throughout their lives?

- Some people believe it's better to spend money on experiences rather than material possessions. Do you agree?

- Should schools focus more on teaching practical skills, such as budgeting and cooking, in addition to academic subjects?

- Is it better to have a few close friends or many acquaintances? Explain your view.

- Discuss the benefits and drawbacks of online education compared to traditional classroom learning.

- Do you believe that celebrities have a responsibility to be role models?

- Should governments restrict the use of cars in city centers to reduce air pollution?

- Young people need to travel and experience different cultures before starting their careers. What's your opinion?

- Are books and printed materials becoming obsolete due to the rise of digital media?

- Discuss the advantages and disadvantages of living in a multicultural society.

- Do you agree or disagree that all students should be required to study art and music in secondary school?

- Should governments invest more in space exploration or addressing pressing Earth issues?

- Is it better to specialize in one field of study or to have a broad knowledge of many subjects?

- Should people be allowed to keep exotic animals as pets?

- Discuss the effects of fast food on people's health and society.

- Do you think that advertising has more positive or negative effects on society?

- Some people believe modern technology makes people more isolated and less friendly. Do you agree?

- Should higher education be free for all students, or should individuals have to pay for their education?

- Do you think traditional art and music forms are still relevant in today's society?

- Discuss the impact of climate change on the environment and what measures should be taken to address it.

- Is it more important to focus on economic growth or environmental protection?

Remember, the key to writing a successful TOEFL essay is to present clear arguments, provide examples and evidence to support your points, and demonstrate a good command of English grammar and vocabulary. Practice writing essays on various topics to become comfortable with the format and expectations of the TOEFL writing section.
