# GenAI to compare revised paragraph

[GenAI prompting sequence for comparing two body paragraphs](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t_lomSgaSDQ)[[GenAI prompting sequence for comparing two body paragraphs|GenAI prompting sequence for comparing two body paragraphs - Notes]]

## Initial prompt

```text
Compare the following two paragraphs and assess any improvements in 1) supporting sentences supporting the main idea; 2) writing coherence that includes appropriate organizational and reasoning patterns; 3) writing cohesion that includes appropriate transitions, use of pronouns, use of direct repetition, and use of hypernyms and hyponyms; review writing mechanics and conventions that include standard grammar and use of vocabulary when writing an academic text; assess subjects being the agents of action in each sentence (i.e., active voice is preferred over passive voice) and avoid overusing verbs like "to be", "to have", and "to make":
```

## Follow-up prompt

```text
In the second paragraph, assess in greater detail in the flow of ideas from one sentence to the next and certain grammar conventions like subject-verb agreement, active vs. passive voice, overusing linking verbs like "to be", whether or not the paragraph focuses on one main idea or point, proper punctuation and capitalization, comma splices, runon sentences, and sentence fragments.
```
