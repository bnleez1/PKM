# (SP) Common spelling mistakes in English

## **Homophones:**

- **Affect/Effect:** Affect (verb) means to influence; Effect (noun) means a result.

- **Their/There/They're:** Their (possessive pronoun); There (adverb, indicating location); They're (contraction of "they are").

- **Your/You're:** Your (possessive pronoun); You're (contraction of "you are").

- **Its/It's:** Its (possessive pronoun); It's (contraction of "it is").

- **To/Too/Two:** To (preposition); Too (adverb, meaning "also" or "excessively"); Two (number).

- **Accept/Except:** Accept (verb, meaning to receive); Except (preposition, meaning "excluding").

- **Compliment/Complement:** Compliment (expression of praise); Complement (something that completes or enhances).

## **Double Letters:**

- **Embarrassment:** Two "r"s and two "s"s.

- **Accommodation:** Two "c"s and two "m"s.

- **Possession:** Two "s"s in the middle.

- **Necessary:** One "c" and two "s"s.

- **Committee:** Two "m"s, two "t"s, and two "e"s.

## **Silent Letters:**

- **Wednesday:** The "d" is silent.

- **Island:** The "s" is silent.

- **Knife:** The "k" is silent.

- **Gnaw:** The "g" is silent.

## **Other Common Mistakes:**

- **Definitely:** Often misspelled as "definately."

- **Separate:** Often misspelled as "seperate."

- **A lot:** Two words, not one ("alot").

- **Believe:** Remember "i" before "e," except after "c."

- **License:** Often misspelled as "lisence."

- **Maintenance:** Often misspelled as "maintainence."

- **Receive:** Remember "e" before "i," except after "c."

- **Argument:** Often misspelled as "arguement."

## **Tips for Avoiding Spelling Mistakes:**

- **Read regularly:** This will help you become more familiar with correct spelling.

- **Use a dictionary or spell checker:** These tools can help you identify and correct mistakes.

- **Learn common spelling rules:** Many spelling rules can help you remember how to spell words correctly.

- **Break down words into syllables:** This can make it easier to spell long or difficult words.

- **Practice spelling words that you often misspell:** This will help you improve your spelling skills.
