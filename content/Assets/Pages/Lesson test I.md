# Lesson test I

Here is a 50-minute lesson plan for teaching English language learners about social media:

Title: Exploring Social Media

Grade Level: Adult English Language Learners (Intermediate to Advanced)

Time: 50 minutes

Objectives:

- Students will be able to understand and use vocabulary related to social media.

- Students will be able to identify and explain the different types of social media platforms.

- Students will be able to use social media responsibly and safely.

Materials:

- Whiteboard and markers

- Handouts with social media vocabulary and guided discussion questions

- Computer or mobile device with internet access

- Social media platforms (e.g. Facebook, Twitter, Instagram, LinkedIn)

Warm-Up (5 minutes):

- Begin by asking students if they use social media and which platforms they use.

- Write their responses on the board and encourage students to share their experiences.

Vocabulary (15 minutes):

- Distribute handouts with social media vocabulary and have students review and discuss the words in small groups.

- Write the words on the board and provide definitions and examples.

- Focus on the following vocabulary:

    - Social media

    - Platform

    - Post

    - Share

    - Follow

    - Like

    - Comment

    - Hashtag

Guided Discussion (15 minutes):

- Ask students to discuss the following questions in small groups:

    - What are the benefits of using social media?

    - What are the risks of using social media?

    - How can you use social media responsibly and safely?

- Encourage students to use the vocabulary they just learned in their discussions.

Social Media Exploration (20 minutes):

- Have students explore different social media platforms on their computer or mobile device.

- Encourage them to create an account on a platform they are interested in and explore its features.

- Have students share their experiences with the class and discuss the similarities and differences between the different platforms.

Best Practices (10 minutes):

- Discuss the following best practices for using social media responsibly and safely:

    - Be careful what you post online.

    - Don't share personal information online.

    - Don't post anything that could be considered offensive or inappropriate.

    - Be respectful of others online.

- Encourage students to practice these best practices when using social media.
