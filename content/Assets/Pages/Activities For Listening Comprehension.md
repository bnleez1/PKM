# Activities For Listening Comprehension 

- Repetition

    - To hear sound patterns

        - Rhyming poems

        - Songs

        - Couplets

        - Tongue twisters

        - Jingles

        - Alliterative poems and books

    - To listen to sentences

        - Jazz chants

        - Dialogues

        - Skits

- Understanding

    - Listening to answer factual questions orally or in writing

        - Dialogues

        - Talks

        - Lectures

        - Arguments

    - Listening to take notes

        - Support an argument

        - Persuade

- Communication

    - Playing games

        - Twenty questions

        - Pictionary

        - Password

        - Simon Says

        - Mother, May I?

    - Open-ended sentences

    - Conversation starters

    - Cooperative problem-solving activities

        - Riddles

        - Logic puzzles

        - Brain teasers
