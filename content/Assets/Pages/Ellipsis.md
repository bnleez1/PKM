# Ellipsis

**In-Practice Example:**
Teaching learners how to sound more natural by dropping redundant words (e.g., 'Do you want to go?' 'Yes, I want to [go]').

**Common Misconception:**
Ellipsis is a grammatical error or 'lazy' speech.
