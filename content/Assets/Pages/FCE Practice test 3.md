### FCE Use of English Practice Test

#### Part 1: Multiple Choice Cloze

For questions 1-8, read the text below and choose the correct answer (A, B, C or D).

**A Changing City**

London is a fascinating city which (1) ___ millions of visitors every year. People come to see its famous landmarks and experience its diverse culture. However, the city (2) ___ dramatically over the past decades. Neighborhoods have been transformed, and new buildings now stand (3) ___ traditional ones. Many locals believe that while change is (4) ___, it is important to maintain London's unique character. Tourists, however, (5) ___ these changes as exciting and innovative. The mix of old and new (6) ___ London an appealing destination, keeping it vibrant and constantly (7) ___. Undoubtedly, this city will continue to evolve and surprise its visitors for many years (8) ___.

1. A. attracts B. invites C. welcomes D. receives

2. A. developed B. has changed C. altered D. improved

3. A. close B. besides C. beside D. among

4. A. essential B. usual C. normal D. ordinary

5. A. regard B. think C. suppose D. consider

6. A. makes B. turns C. gets D. becomes

7. A. growing B. progressing C. moving D. advancing

8. A. coming B. ahead C. forward D. future

#### Part 3: Word Formation

For questions 17-24, read the text below. Use the word given in capitals to form a word that fits in the gap.

**Exploring the Universe**

Humans have always been (17) ___ (FASCINATE) by the universe. The (18) ___ (DISCOVER) of planets beyond our solar system has significantly increased our knowledge and (19) ___ (CURIOUS). Scientists now have better (20) ___ (EQUIP) to study distant stars, making it possible to learn more about the potential for life elsewhere. Astronomers' (21) ___ (EXCITE) grows with each new finding, and public (22) ___ (INTERESTING) in space exploration remains high. Space agencies around the world continue to invest (23) ___ (HEAVY) in research missions, aiming to unlock the secrets of our vast and (24) ___ (MYSTERY) universe.

#### Part 4: Key Word Transformations

For questions 25-30, complete the second sentence so that it has a similar meaning to the first sentence, using the word given. Do not change the word given. You must use between two and five words, including the word given.

1. The weather was too cold for us to go swimming. **ENOUGH**

```text
The weather \_\_\_ for us to go swimming.
```

1. Sarah started learning French three years ago. **BEEN**

```text
Sarah \_\_\_ three years.
```

1. I haven't seen a better film than this one. **BEST**

```text
This is \_\_\_ seen.
```

1. My brother doesn't like sports as much as I do. **KEEN**

```text
My brother \_\_\_ sports as I am.
```

1. "Did you finish your homework?" asked my mother. **IF**

```text
My mother asked \_\_\_ my homework.
```

1. They cancelled the concert because of the rain. **CALLED**

```text
The concert \_\_\_ because of the rain.
```
