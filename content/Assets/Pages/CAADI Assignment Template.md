# CAADI Assignment Template

## Purpose

Use this open assignment to complete independent English practice in the **CAADI self-access center**. You will choose a worksheet that addresses an area of English you want or need to improve and complete it independently.

## What You Need to Do

1. **Choose one CAADI worksheet** that is appropriate for your course and current learning needs.

    - **Writing I:** choose a worksheet that helps you improve skills such as sentence construction, paragraph development, vocabulary, grammar in context, organization, cohesion, drafting, revising, or editing.

    - **Listening I:** choose a worksheet that helps you improve skills such as predicting, listening for gist, listening for specific information, vocabulary recognition, identifying key words, understanding speakers, inference, or listening strategies.

2. **Complete the worksheet independently in CAADI.** Follow all instructions provided on the worksheet and use the available self-access resources as needed.

3. Include your full name and date you completed the worksheet on the worksheet itself.

4. Consult with a CAADI Tutor for feedback and support, making sure you get the signature and CAADI stamp on your worksheet.

5. **Take a picture of your completed worksheet.**

6. **Submit your worksheet to the appropriate assignment in Microsft Teams.**

## Short Reflection

Respond briefly in English.

1. What did you practice in this worksheet?

2. What was easy for you?

3. What was difficult for you?

4. What is one thing you learned or improved?

5. What would you like to practice next?

## Evidence to Submit

Submit the evidence required by your instructor. This may include:

- the completed CAADI worksheet;

- a clear photo, scan, or digital copy of the completed work;

- any required audio, written response, or activity record; and

- the completed short reflection.

## Completion Criteria

This assignment is considered complete when you:

- choose a worksheet relevant to your course and learning needs;

- complete the worksheet with reasonable effort and independently;

- provide the required evidence of completion; and

- complete the reflection thoughtfully in English.

## Use of AI and Other Support

Use dictionaries, reference materials, CAADI resources, and digital tools only when they support the learning goal of the worksheet. **Do not use GenAI to complete the worksheet for you.** If a worksheet explicitly permits GenAI, follow the instructions provided and document your use when required.

> **Instructor note:** Duplicate this Assignment for each CAADI open assignment. Add the due date and, when appropriate, connect it to the relevant Unit or Module. Adjust the evidence requirements if a particular worksheet requires audio, screenshots, or another format.
