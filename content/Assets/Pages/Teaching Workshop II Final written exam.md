# Teaching Workshop II: Final written exam

# Instructions

1. Based on the rubric below, answer each of the following questions by writing a four-five coherent and cohesive reflective paragraph (i.e., four paragraphs in total).

    1. What listening strategies did you and/or your students employ during your lesson and how challenges and/or successes did you have? What listening strategies could you have done instead for better results (i.e., of learning outcomes)?

    2. What speaking strategies did you and/or your students employ during your lesson and how challenges and/or successes did you have? What speaking strategies could you have done instead for better results (i.e., of learning outcomes)?

    3. What reading strategies did you and/or your students employ during your lesson and how challenges and/or successes did you have? What reading strategies could you have done instead for better results (i.e., of learning outcomes)?

    4. What writing strategies did you and/or your students employ during your lesson and how challenges and/or successes did you have? What writing strategies could you have done instead for better results (i.e., of learning outcomes)?

2. Upload your paragraphs to the [virtual classroom](https://aulavirtual.uaa.mx/mod/assign/view.php?id=472579).

# Rubric

Criteria

Exceptional (4 points)

Proficient (3 points)

Developing (2 points)

Emerging (1 point)

Listening Strategies

Demonstrates a comprehensive understanding of various listening strategies and effectively employs them to enhance comprehension. Students actively engage with the listening material, utilizing strategies such as predicting, summarizing, and clarifying.

Demonstrates a good understanding of listening strategies and applies them to support comprehension. Students engage with the listening material, but may need additional prompts or clarification.

Shows some understanding of listening strategies and attempts to apply them. Students may struggle to engage with the listening material and may require significant support.

Has limited understanding of listening strategies and struggles to apply them effectively. Students may find it difficult to follow the listening material and may need extensive support.

Speaking Strategies

Employs a variety of speaking strategies to communicate effectively and confidently. Students articulate their ideas clearly, using appropriate vocabulary and grammar. They actively participate in discussions and adapt their communication style to different audiences.

Demonstrates a good grasp of speaking strategies and uses them to communicate effectively. Students express their ideas clearly, but may need some support with vocabulary or grammar. They participate in discussions and show some adaptability to different audiences.

Shows some understanding of speaking strategies and attempts to use them in communication. Students may struggle to express their ideas clearly and may need significant support with vocabulary or grammar. They may have difficulty participating in discussions and may not adapt their communication style to different audiences.

Has limited understanding of speaking strategies and struggles to apply them effectively in communication. Students may have difficulty expressing their ideas clearly and may require extensive support with vocabulary or grammar. They may not participate in discussions and may not adapt their communication style to different audiences.

Reading Strategies

Effectively employs a variety of reading strategies to enhance comprehension and fluency. Students actively engage with the text, using strategies such as previewing, predicting, and summarizing. They can identify and analyze key details, main ideas, and supporting evidence.

Demonstrates a good understanding of reading strategies and applies them to support comprehension and fluency. Students engage with the text, but may need some additional prompts or clarification. They can identify key details and main ideas, but may struggle with analyzing supporting evidence.

Shows some understanding of reading strategies and attempts to apply them. Students may struggle to engage with the text and may require significant support to identify key details and main ideas. They may have difficulty analyzing supporting evidence.

Has limited understanding of reading strategies and struggles to apply them effectively. Students may find it difficult to engage with the text and may need extensive support to identify key details and main ideas. They may not be able to analyze supporting evidence.

Writing Strategies

Demonstrates a comprehensive understanding of various writing strategies and effectively employs them to produce clear, organized, and well-supported written work. Students use appropriate vocabulary and grammar, and they effectively revise and edit their work.

Demonstrates a good understanding of writing strategies and applies them to produce clear, organized, and supported written work. Students may need some additional support with vocabulary or grammar, but they can revise and edit their work effectively.

Shows some understanding of writing strategies and attempts to apply them. Students may struggle to produce clear, organized, and supported written work. They may need significant support with vocabulary or grammar, and they may have difficulty revising and editing their work.

Has limited understanding of writing strategies and struggles to apply them effectively. Students may have difficulty producing clear, organized, and supported written work. They may require extensive support with vocabulary or grammar, and they may not be able to revise and edit their work effectively.
