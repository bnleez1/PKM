# GenAI Terms

Common terms include:

- **AI-assisted** — GenAI helped with brainstorming, outlining, revising, or editing, but a person remained the primary author.

- **AI-generated** — substantial text, images, audio, code, or other content was produced by GenAI.

- **AI-enhanced** — existing human-created material was improved, expanded, reformatted, or polished using AI.

- **AI-edited** — AI was used for grammar, clarity, style, organization, or proofreading.

- **AI-supported** — a broad term indicating that AI contributed to the work process.

- **AI-mediated** — AI played an intermediary role, such as translating, summarizing, adapting, or transforming content.

- **AI-translated** — GenAI was used to translate the material.

- **AI-summarized** — GenAI was used to create a summary.

- **AI-adapted** — AI helped modify content for a different audience, level, format, or purpose.

- **Human-authored, AI-assisted** — emphasizes that the author retained control and responsibility.

- **Human-reviewed AI-generated content** — indicates that AI produced material that was later checked and revised by a person.

The acknowledgment itself may be called an:

- **AI disclosure statement**

- **Generative AI use statement**

- **AI transparency statement**

- **AI acknowledgment**

- **AI attribution statement**

- **AI provenance statement**

- **Statement of AI assistance**

- **Declaration of generative AI use**

A practical classification for a GenAI policy could be:

1. **No AI used**

2. **AI-assisted**

3. **AI-edited**

4. **AI-generated and human-reviewed**

5. **Substantially AI-generated**

Example wording:

> **Generative AI use statement:** ChatGPT was used to brainstorm ideas and improve clarity. The author reviewed, revised, and takes responsibility for the final content.

For an image:

> **AI-generated image:** Created using an image-generation model and selected and edited by the author.

For substantial text generation:

> Portions of this document were generated with ChatGPT. The author verified the information, revised the output, and accepts responsibility for the final version.

The most neutral umbrella term is **“AI-assisted content.”** The clearest formal label is **“Generative AI use statement.”**
