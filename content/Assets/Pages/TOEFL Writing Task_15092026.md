# TOEFL Writing Task_15092026 in [[60 Public/Website/Current Courses/Writing I/Modules/W1 Module 06 Connecting Everyday Experiences|W1 Module 06 Connecting Everyday Experiences]]

TOEFL ITP itself does not include an essay-writing section, but you can use a **TOEFL-style independent writing prompt** modeled on the kind of opinion task traditionally associated with TOEFL writing. For your Writing I learners, I would reduce the length and linguistic demands to match the course’s A2 target while retaining the core requirement of stating a position, supporting it with reasons, and giving examples. This fits the course emphasis on organized paragraphs, connectors, and increasingly independent student writing.

## TOEFL-Style Writing Task

### Study Alone or Study with Others?

Some university students prefer to **study alone**. Other students prefer to **study with classmates or friends**.

**Which do you prefer?**

Write about your preference and explain **why** you prefer it. Give reasons and examples from your own experience.

You may consider questions such as:

- Where do you usually study?
- Do other people help you learn?
- Is it easier for you to concentrate alone or with others?
- What are the advantages of your preferred way of studying?
- Can you give an example from your university experience?

### Your Task

Write **one well-developed paragraph of approximately 120–150 words**.

Your paragraph should include:

1. A clear **topic sentence** stating your preference.
2. At least **two reasons** supporting your opinion.
3. At least **one specific example**.
4. Connectors such as **because, also, for example, but, so, first,** or **finally**.
5. A **concluding sentence** that restates or summarizes your main idea.

Write independently. Focus first on communicating your ideas clearly rather than trying to use difficult vocabulary.
