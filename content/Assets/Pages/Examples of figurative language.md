# Examples of figurative language

- 1. Simile: Her smile is as bright as the sun.

- 2. Metaphor: He is a shining star in the classroom.

- 3. Personification: The wind whispered through the trees.

- 4. Onomatopoeia: The thunder boomed loudly in the distance.

- 5. Oxymoron: The silence was deafening.

- 6. Hyperbole: I've told you a million times not to do that.

- 7. Allusion: She was a modern-day Cinderella, waiting for her prince to arrive.

- 8. Idiom: It's raining cats and dogs outside.

- 9. Imagery: The aroma of freshly baked bread wafted through the air.

- 10. Symbolism: The dove represents peace and harmony.

- 11. Alliteration: Peter Piper picked a peck of pickled peppers.

- 12. Assonance: The rain in Spain falls mainly on the plain.

- 13. Consonance: Mike likes his bike and hikes in the countryside.

- 14. Metonymy: The White House issued a statement today.

- 15. Synecdoche: She asked for her hand in marriage.

- 16. Irony: The fire station burned down.

- 17. Sarcasm: "Oh, great! Another rainy day!" he said with a sarcastic tone.

- 18. Litotes: The party was not too bad.

- 19. Pun: I used to be a baker, but I couldn't make enough dough.

- 20. Anaphora: We will fight for justice. We will fight for equality. We will fight for our rights.

- 21. Tautology: The final outcome is yet to be determined.

- 22. Understatement: It's just a small scratch; it's nothing serious.

- These examples showcase different types of figurative language used in literature and everyday language.
