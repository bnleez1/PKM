# Writing I Student Reflections Summary

> [!summary] Summary
> Summary of student reflections from October 7, 2026

# Improvements

- **Paragraph organization and focus:** Students describe moving from scattered ideas to paragraphs centered on one main idea, with supporting details in a more logical order. Several credit brainstorming and outlines.
    
- **Vocabulary and expression:** Many report knowing more words, repeating themselves less, and expressing their meaning more clearly.
    
- **Topic sentences:** Several students now understand how a topic sentence introduces and limits the paragraph’s focus.
    
- **Sentence construction:** Students report better understanding of complete sentences and the differences among simple, compound, and complex sentences.
    
- **Punctuation and revision:** Some describe improved use of periods, commas, and capitals, alongside checking their work before submitting it.
    
- **Confidence:** Many feel more comfortable writing in English and recognize that regular practice has helped.
    

# Goals

- **Use punctuation consistently:** Students want to identify sentence boundaries and understand when to use commas and periods.
    
- **Write stronger topic sentences and supporting details:** They want more specific main ideas and relevant explanations that develop those ideas.
    
- **Choose connectors accurately:** Several still struggle to select connecting words that express the intended relationship between ideas.
    
- **Strengthen grammar and sentence structure:** Goals include mastering complex sentences, avoiding incomplete sentences, and improving verb forms, subject–verb agreement, plurals, and spelling.
    
- **Expand usable vocabulary:** Students want to choose precise words more easily and reduce dependence on translating from Spanish.
    
- **Write more fluently and independently:** Some want longer, more developed paragraphs; others want greater concision and speed. Students also mention proofreading, comparing earlier and recent work, and practicing regularly.
