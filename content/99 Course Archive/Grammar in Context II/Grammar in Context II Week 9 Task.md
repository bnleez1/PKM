# Task

- Rename one of the blank Word documents in [Week 9](https://eduuaa.sharepoint.com/:f:/s/GrammarInContextII2/EhQmM63hPcREhHEaaU3t3H8BLg7jISo9KqT1Wwqzb-Q6kg?e=gXaDVK) and rename it using your full name.

- Using the paragraph you completed on Monday as a guide, write a paragraph about you and two other people close to you regarding your future plans. 

    - Include three conditionals (sentences) that express a future possibility. 

    - Each sentence should include 12-18 words and the paragraph should have at least 100 words.

    - Your paragraph should include at least one example of the following: one simple, compound, and complex sentence, participial phrase, gerund phrase, and infinitive phrase.

    - Consider prepositional phrases as well if you are trying to add more content (words) to a sentence.

    - Include at least two transitions throughout your 100-word paragraph: [[Types of transitions]].

    - Avoid using any translation software and any software that checks grammar like Microsoft Editor, Grammarly, etc.

    - Highlight any participial phrases in yellow, gerund phrases in green, infinitive phrases in blue, and condition sentences in pink.

    - Avoid working with a writing/grammar partner to check each other's work.
