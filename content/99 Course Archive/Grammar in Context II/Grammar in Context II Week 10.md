- Review first conditional and (restricted and unrestrictive) relative clauses with new vocabulary.

    - Restricted relative clauses are a necessary part of the sentence - they are required for the sentence to make sense. Unrestricted relative clauses provide extra information. The sentence still makes sense if the unrestricted relative clause were to be removed.

    - How should the comma be used (if at all) in each of the following sentences below? 

        - My biological mother, who has red hair, also enjoys getting her fingernails done. Unrestricted

        - The recipe that I used for making chocolate chip cookies called for different ingredients. Restricted

        - We need to hire someone who understands digital marketing. Restricted

        - Is this the platform where the train to Edinburgh departs? Restricted

        - The report, which contained several errors, had to be rewritten. Unrestricted

        - My friend Alicia, who is a talented artist, painted this picture. Unrestricted

    - In your teams for this week, choose a product that you want to review. Send me a list of your teammates and the product you wish to review. Write two sentences with relative clauses - per person - and include one example of a restricted and one example of an unrestricted clause.
