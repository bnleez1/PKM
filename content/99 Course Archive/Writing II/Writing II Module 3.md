        2. Khan (2023) suggests that AI has the potential to bring about the most significant positive change in education history.

        3. AI has the potential to bring about the most significant positive change in education history (Khan, 2023).

    - Example 2

        1. “AI tools are useful in brainstorming or thinking outside the box, because you can get large language models to perform as randomness generators” (Harvard Law School, 2024).

        3. AI tools can help generate creative ideas by acting as sources of randomness (Harvard Law School, 2024).

    - Example 3

        1. “Teachers are already integrating AI into the classroom in fun, engaging, and academically rich ways—by having students fact-check ChatGPT’s responses” (Edutopia, 2023).

        2. Edutopia (2023) notes that teachers are already using AI in engaging and educational ways, such as having students verify the accuracy of ChatGPT’s answers.

        3. Teachers are already using AI in engaging and educational ways, such as having students verify the accuracy of ChatGPT’s answers (Edutopia, 2023).

    - Example 4

        1. “A.I. will force us humans to double down on those talents and skills that only humans possess” (New York Times, 2023).

        2. The New York Times (2023) argues that AI will push humans to focus more on uniquely human skills and talents.

        3. AI will push humans to focus more on uniquely human skills and talents (New York Times, 2023).

    - Example 5

        1. “These tools can function like personal assistants: Ask ChatGPT to create a study schedule, simplify a complex idea, or suggest topics for a research paper” (Chronicle of Higher Education, 2023).

        2. The Chronicle of Higher Education (2023) mentions that AI tools can serve as personal assistants, helping with tasks like creating study schedules, simplifying complex concepts, or suggesting research paper topics.

        3. AI tools can serve as personal assistants, helping with tasks like creating study schedules, simplifying complex concepts, or suggesting research paper topics (The Chronicle of Higher Education, 2023).

- Example of a body paragraph

    The abandoned lighthouse on the rocky coast stands as a weathered sentinel, its peeling paint and broken windows telling stories of storms weathered and ships guided safely home.

    The abandoned lighthouse that rises above the sharp rocks keeps watch like an old guard, even though it is falling apart. Lighthouses often stand in dangerous places, like rocky coasts and near harbors, so their lights can warn sailors about danger and help ships find safe water (NOAA National Ocean Service, n.d.). This shows why the peeling paint and broken windows look like signs of many years of wind, salt, and storms. In the past, lighthouses were built to guide ships safely into busy ports and away from hidden rocks (National Park Service, n.d.). Because of this, the lighthouse feels like more than an empty building; it feels like a strong protector with a long history. Even now, its damaged look still suggests it is watching over the coast.
