# Description

Writing II is a practical subject aimed at developing paragraph and essay writing skills in English. This subject is taken simultaneously with Listening Comprehension and Conversation II, Grammar in Context II, Reading II, and Integration of Skills through Culture II, and is preceded by all subjects from Propaedeutic I. # Methodology

The course uses a student-centered methodology with a step-by-step approach that progressively builds foundational writing skills. It starts by exploring the purpose and structure of various essay types, including descriptive, expository, and argumentative. Students then learn to brainstorm and organize ideas through graphic organizers and outlines. Instruction highlights the importance of clear thesis statements and well-structured paragraphs that include topic sentences and supporting evidence. Consistent practice in drafting, revising, and editing fosters continuous improvement. Peer reviews and teacher feedback further refine writing, while targeted lessons on grammar, vocabulary, and transitions enhance clarity and coherence.
