## Instruction

- Tip: Linking sentence transitions for concluding first and second body paragraphs (linking sentence that connects the main idea of the current paragraph to the main idea of the next body paragraph.

    - “While it is true that ***, this view overlooks*** .”

    - “Admittedly, ***; however,*** .”

    - “This argument seems convincing because ***, yet it fails to consider*** .”

    - “Although opponents claim ***, [state the claim (topic sentence) from the next body paragraph]*** .”

    - “Even if ***, it does not follow that*** .”

    - “The key issue is not ***, but rather*** .”

## Instruction

- Complete first draft of Argumentative Essay (block method) in Argumentative Essays. There is nothing to submit to Teams.

    1. Title of your essay

    2. Image

    3. ‣ with at least one citation to support the background information

        1. hook

        2. background information (with at least one citation)

        3. Thesis statement

    4. Three body paragraphs according to the The M.E.A.L. Plan: A Comprehensive Approach to Paragraph Development

        1. At least one citation to support evidence

        2. 120-180 words

        3. 5-8 sentences

    5. Conclusion Paragraph

        1. Restate the thesis statement

        2. State the significance, relevance, big picture, etc. of the thesis statement. Why should anyone care?

        3. Conclude with a closing statement

    6. Citations and references according to APA.

        1. Paraphrase all citations.

        2. Include only parenthetical citations.

        3. Avoid mentioning the author, study, researchers, etc.

        4. At least three references

        5. At least one citation in each paragraph except for the conclusion paragraph.
