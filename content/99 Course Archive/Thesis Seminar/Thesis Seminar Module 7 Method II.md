# Thesis Seminar Module 7: Method II

[Thesis Seminar Recap for Week 7](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sghN-D713Wc)[[Thesis Seminar Recap for Week 7|Thesis Seminar Recap for Week 7 - Notes]]

### 🎯 Understanding

**Instructors will understand that…**

- Effective method sections maintain tight alignment among the thesis/purpose, research questions, and the structure (headings) of the methodology chapter.

- Participant selection tools (e.g., screening questionnaires) and interview protocols must be purpose-built to gather the specific information needed for the study and to justify sampling decisions.

- Ethical access (informed consent, voluntary participation, confidentiality) is not an add-on; it shapes what data can be collected and how it can be reported.

### 🎯 Language Objective

**Students will be able to…**

- check alignment by mapping each research question to a planned data source and a matching methodology subheading (3–6 bullet points).

- draft a short screening questionnaire for selecting participants (6–10 items) using clear, unambiguous wording and consistent response formats.

- draft a semi-structured interview guide (8–12 questions plus prompts) that includes opening, core, and closing questions and uses neutral, non-leading language.

### 🎯 Vocabulary

- alignment (research questions–method)

- methodology chapter / section headings

- participant selection

- screening questionnaire

- sampling criteria

- semi-structured interview

- interview guide / protocol

- prompts / probes

- neutral wording / leading question

- informed consent

- voluntary participation

- confidentiality / anonymity
