# Writing I Week 4 Assignment

## Assignment Overview

**Purpose:** The purpose of this assignment is to strengthen your ability to write and review a clear five-sentence paragraph. By working with a classmate, you will practice identifying clauses and sentence types, checking punctuation and paragraph organization, and recognizing the main idea and supporting ideas while using peer feedback to improve your writing.

**Assignment type:** Pairs

## Instructions

1. Complete outline.

2. Complete five-sentence paragraph. 

3. Exchange notebooks.

4. Add to your classmate's notebook today's date and *Review by* [your name].

5. Review classmate's body paragraph from yesterday

    1. Identify each clause (subject + verb) and mark using parentheses. For example, (*Tom threw) the ball*. 

    2. Check to see there are five sentences and that each sentence concludes with a period (full stop). No question or exclamation marks.

    3. Identify each simple, compound, and complex sentence. See [Mastering sentence types: A guide to simple, compound, complex, and compound-complex sentences](../Pages/Mastering%20sentence%20types%20A%20guide%20to%20simple,%20compound,%20complex,%20and%20compound-com.md) for more.

    4. Answer the following sentence stems:

        1. I think the main idea is...

        2. The three most important supporting ideas are...

        3. After reading my classmate's paragraph, I understand...

6. Return text to your classmate.

7. Take a picture of your body paragraph along with the comments made by your classmate.

8. Submit your image to Teams assignment.
