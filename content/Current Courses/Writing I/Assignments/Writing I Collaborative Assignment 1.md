# Writing I Collaborative Assignment 1

**Purpose:**  
In this collaborative writing workshop, you will plan, draft, review, and revise an original paragraph about **study habits**. The purpose is to practice writing as a process rather than producing only a final paragraph. You will work with classmates to examine organization, clarity, connectors, sentence structure, punctuation, and vocabulary while maintaining responsibility for your own writing. This assignment supports Unit II goals related to writing simple narrative and descriptive paragraphs, using sequence and lexical cohesion, and revising an independently written first draft through feedback.

**Assignment type:** Individual + Group + In-class
**Estimated time:** Approximately 3 hours
## Learning Outcomes

By completing this assignment, students will be able to:

- Plan and write a clear, organized A2-level paragraph about a study habit or study routine.
- Use appropriate supporting details, connectors, sentence structures, vocabulary, and punctuation to develop a main idea.
- Give and evaluate peer feedback and use relevant feedback to revise an independently written first draft.
- Explain important revision decisions while maintaining individual authorship of the final paragraph.
    
## Task

Complete a collaborative **Paragraph Studio** focused on the topic of **study habits**.

You will select one study habit or study routine, organize your ideas, and independently write a **8-10 sentence paragraph**. You will then work with classmates to review the paragraph from different perspectives, discuss possible improvements, and decide which feedback to use.
