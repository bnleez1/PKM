# Assignment Overview

**Purpose:**  
In this collaborative writing workshop, you will plan, draft, review, and revise an original paragraph about **study habits**. The purpose is to practice writing as a process rather than producing only a final paragraph. You will work with classmates to examine organization, clarity, connectors, sentence structure, punctuation, and vocabulary while maintaining responsibility for your own writing. This assignment supports Unit II goals related to writing simple narrative and descriptive paragraphs, using sequence and lexical cohesion, and revising an independently written first draft through feedback.

**Assignment type:** Individual + Group + In-class
**Estimated time:** Approximately 3 hours
## Learning Outcomes

By completing this assignment, students will be able to:

- Plan and write a clear, organized A2-level paragraph about a study habit or study routine.
- Use appropriate supporting details, connectors, sentence structures, vocabulary, and punctuation to develop a main idea.
- Give and evaluate peer feedback and use relevant feedback to revise an independently written first draft.
- Explain important revision decisions while maintaining individual authorship of the final paragraph.
    
## Task

Complete a collaborative **Paragraph Studio** focused on the topic of **study habits**.

You will select one study habit or study routine, organize your ideas, and independently write a **7–9 sentence paragraph**. You will then work with classmates to review the paragraph from different perspectives, discuss possible improvements, and decide which feedback to use.

Your final product will be a **Writing Workshop Portfolio** showing the development of your paragraph from planning through revision.

## Instructions

1. **Choose and plan your topic.**  
    Select one specific study habit or routine, such as preparing for an exam, reviewing vocabulary, taking notes, completing homework, studying with classmates, organizing your schedule, or avoiding distractions. Complete the graphic organizer provided in class to identify your main idea and supporting details.
    
    
## Deliverable to Teams Assignment

Submit **one Writing Workshop Portfolio** containing:

1. Your completed graphic organize
2. Draft 1 of your paragraph
3. Your peer-review feedback
4. Your GenAI feedback record, **only if GenAI was authorized and used**
5. Draft 2 — your revised **7–9 sentence paragraph**
6. Your **3–4 sentence revision reflection**

Your submission should show evidence of the complete writing process: **planning → drafting → feedback → revision → reflection**.
