# Course Description

This theoretical and practical course focuses on developing English listening comprehension skills from beginner to CEFR level A2. It contributes to English language learning through the implementation and use of techniques designed to improve listening comprehension in an interactive and communicative manner. The course is taken concurrently with Basic English Grammar, Basic English Vocabulary, Basic English Conversation, Basic English Reading, and Basic English Writing, and is a prerequisite for Intermediate English Listening Comprehension.

# Course Objective

At the end of the course, the student will use strategies that promote the development of their English listening comprehension for appropriate communication and interaction in that language at a CEFR level A2, with openness to diverse cultural contexts, as well as pluralism, respect, and equity.
