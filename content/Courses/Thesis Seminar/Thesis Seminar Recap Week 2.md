## Context

- For those taking [[Thesis Seminar]] - notes from Week 1.

    - For those who have a working version of a literature review (e.g., )from Academic Writing).

        - Review research questions to see how they align with potential participants: [[Situational questions when doing research]] and [[Finding participants]].

        - Determine where a survey, interview, or focus group is needed to get more information about potential participants - to inform research questions and any changes needed to the literature review.

        - Make necessary changes to literature review.

        - Review [[Narrowing down a topic]], [Organizing an effective argument: Methods and examples](../Pages/Organizing%20an%20effective%20argument%20Methods%20and%20examples.md), & [[Organizational Patterns - Text Structure]]
