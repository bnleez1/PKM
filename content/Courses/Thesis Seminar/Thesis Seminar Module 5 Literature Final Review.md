# Thesis Seminar Module 5: Literature Final Review

[Thesis Seminar Recap #5](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=itbsHTypZ8E)[[Thesis Seminar Recap #5|Thesis Seminar Recap #5 - Notes]]

### 🎯 Understanding

**Instructors will understand that…**

- **Coherence and cohesion transform disconnected sections into a unified argument**: coherence ensures every paragraph connects logically to the thesis and research questions, while cohesion uses consistent terminology and explicit transitions to guide readers through the argument's flow.

- **A transitional paragraph bridges two worlds**—it synthesizes what the literature reveals about the problem and explicitly sets up the methodological choices that follow, making clear why your study design is the logical response to gaps or tensions in existing research.

- **Alignment verification is the final quality check**: when thesis key points, section headings, research questions, and planned data collection all map onto each other, the literature review demonstrates that your study is necessary, focused, and feasible.

### 🎯 Language Objective

**Students will be able to…**

- **Revise** the full literature review to ensure every paragraph includes explicit connections to the thesis and research questions, using cohesive devices (pronouns, repeated key terms, transitions) to link ideas within and between sections.

- **Compose** a transitional paragraph (150-200 words) that synthesizes major findings or gaps from the literature review and explicitly bridges to the method section by explaining why your study's approach is warranted.

- **Verify** complete alignment by creating a visual check: thesis key points = section headings = research questions = planned data collection, and revise any mismatches before final submission.

### 🎯 Vocabulary

- **Coherence** — the logical connection between ideas across the entire literature review, ensuring every paragraph advances the argument and connects to thesis and research questions

- **Cohesion** — the linguistic devices that bind sentences and paragraphs together (transitions, consistent terminology, pronouns, repetition of key terms)

- **Transitional paragraph** — a bridge paragraph between literature review and method that synthesizes findings/gaps and explains why your methodological approach logically follows

- **Alignment verification** — systematically checking that thesis key points, section headings, research questions, and study design all correspond to each other

- **Formal academic style** — objective tone, third-person perspective, precise terminology, hedged claims, and adherence to disciplinary conventions (APA)
