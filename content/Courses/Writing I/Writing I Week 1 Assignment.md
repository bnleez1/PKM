# Writing I Week 1 Assignment

## Assignment Overview

**Purpose:** Getting to know you...

**Assignment type:** Individual

## Instructions

1. Complete brainstorm activity.

2. Complete graphic organizer.

3. Complete sentence types: [Mastering sentence types: A guide to simple, compound, complex, and compound-complex sentences](../Pages/Mastering%20sentence%20types%20A%20guide%20to%20simple,%20compound,%20complex,%20and%20compound-com.md)

4. Create paragraph and receive peer feedback.

5. Choose any one of the Word documents in [Week 1.](https://eduuaa.sharepoint.com/:f:/s/WritingI2026/IgDf4jH3rZkmTLRd42qkU725AeIwtDzATYAiuxsDeoxQpW8?e=pgrvxi)in Teams and rename it with your full name, first name first.

6. Insert a picture of your paragraph from your notebook to your Word document in [Week 1.](https://eduuaa.sharepoint.com/:f:/s/WritingI2026/IgDf4jH3rZkmTLRd42qkU725AeIwtDzATYAiuxsDeoxQpW8?e=pgrvxi)

7. Above the picture of your paragraph picture, insert a picture of yourself.

8. Submit the Word document to Teams as an attachment and type out your paragraph in the text box before submitting your assignment (in Teams).

9. Request feedback from your instructor as needed.

## Deliverable to Teams Assignment

1. Attached Word document to Writing I Week 1 Assignment.

2. Submit text of your paragraph for this week.
