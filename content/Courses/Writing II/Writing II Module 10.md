# Writing II Module 10

## Monday

## Instruction

- **Main idea:** Start with one sentence that directly supports your thesis.

- **Evidence:** Paraphrase (or quote when needed) and include an APA-style citation with a signal phrase.

- **Analysis:** Explain the meaning and importance of the evidence (answer *How does this prove the point? Why should the reader care?*).

- **Link:** End by connecting forward to the next paragraph’s idea.

- **Target length:** 5–8 sentences (about 125–175 words).


💡

Takeaway: In an expository essay, your job is to teach the reader—so every paragraph should do three things: state one clear point, support it with cited evidence, and explain why that evidence matters.
